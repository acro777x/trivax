# PLAN V2 — Section-by-Section Plan

## S0 Global Shell & Navigation
The global shell features the new liquid-glass navigation bar and unified background structure. On desktop (viewport 900px+), the header is a floating pill capsule built with `GlassSurface` with SVG refraction in Chromium (and frosted CSS fallback elsewhere), 1px specular highlight border, translucent white body (backgroundOpacity 0.12-0.16), real Kavirox vector logo mark with live wordmark, centered Gooey Nav links (Products, Services, Solutions, Process, FAQ, Contact) with a translucent white gooey blob, and a primary lime "Discuss a project" action pill. On mobile/tablet (<900px), the header is an inset frosted glass bar with the Kavirox logo and a circular 44px glass toggle that activates the Staggered Menu panel containing numbered links and product groups. The chat launcher sits at bottom-right as a compact 48px round icon button on mobile (pill on desktop) with safe-area offset, hiding automatically when contact form fields focus or mobile menus open. A 2px lime scroll progress bar tracks reading depth along the top viewport edge.

## S0.1 Preloader & Intro
The preloader runs full-screen once per session (guarded by sessionStorage and bypassed with reduced motion or `?qa=1`), waiting for preloaded fonts (`Bricolage Grotesque` and `Geist Mono`) and hero ASCII initialization (1.4s minimum to 3.2s cap). A dark canvas with a subtle emerald-lime radial glow presents the SVG mark drawing via GSAP stroke-dashoffset animation, live wordmark letter stagger, wide-tracked `.SPACE` reveal, and a real tabular numeric counter (0–100) on a hairline lime rule. Upon completion, the logo scales down slightly while the panel curtains split vertically (700ms inout transform), seamlessly handing off into Hero Timeline A without FOUC or scroll jitter.

## S1 Hero
The hero uses a centered, symmetrical flex composition within a max-width 880px column over 100svh. Content flows from a sentence-case eyebrow pill ("Product-led technology company") down to an optical-size-tuned Bricolage Grotesque H1 ("Products that solve problems.") featuring the measured, unclipped Stroke Text accent on "solve", followed by a quieter lead line ("Technology that moves businesses forward."), a concise 2-sentence paragraph, and a dual CTA cluster (primary lime Glow Button "Explore Products" and secondary Specular Button "Discuss a Project"). At the base sits the letter-spaced brand line "BUILD. SECURE. GROW." alongside "Incubated at AIC-GBU, Greater Noida". The background utilizes Gradient ASCII tuned to a calmer top intensity with a soft horizon glow behind the headline and radial vignette; on mobile, buttons stack full-width (max 320px) with touch-optimized sizing, and ASCII runs in lite mode.

## S2 Marquee ("Technologies we build with")
Directly beneath the hero, a dedicated monochrome logo loop marquee showcases 10–12 technology logos flanked by edge fade masks with a centered title "Technologies we build with". Hovering on desktop shifts individual icons to primary lime with smooth transitions. On capable desktop devices, it runs via LogoLoop; on mobile and lite devices, it transitions to a pure GPU-accelerated CSS transform loop, ensuring 60–120fps scrolling without thread overhead.

## S3 Products Section
A new flagship section (`#products`) displaying the seven verified Kavirox products and platforms. Desktop displays the featured trio (Rakshak Setu, GBU One, Study Hub AI) as tall 4:5 cards with dark phone mocks, Star Border highlights, and Pixel Swap hover previews (for Rakshak Setu and GBU One), followed by a 4-up compact grid for GBU-SDSM, FitFuel, Try-Out, and LAPS One with Shine Border hover treatments. Products lacking live captures feature stylized, non-fake CSS illustrations with a "Preview coming" tag. On mobile, the featured trio operates as a touch-friendly CSS scroll-snap rail (1.15 cards visible, momentum scroll) with the remaining four in a 2-up grid. Each card displays honest status badges, clear problem-solution copy, and clicking opens the scene-style detail page.

## S3.1 Product Detail Pages & Scene Transition
Dynamic client-side detail pages (`/products/:slug`) rendered with shared-element scene opening transitions. On card activation, the phone mock expands via GSAP Flip into a hero stage view, illuminating a soft lime ambient backdrop while masked headers, honest lifecycle badges, and spec facts cascade in. Detail pages include stage phone hero, problem statement, feature group grid with Lucide icons, screen gallery (with Scroll Expand lead asset where available or pinned horizontal screen scroller), "Where this stands" transparency box (what works vs. what is not included yet), technology chips, and contextual pre-written email CTAs. Lite devices and reduced motion gracefully degrade to swift fade/translate reveals.

## S4 How You Can Work With Us
A structured 4-card workflow section directly introducing the engagement avenues: Ready-built product, Product implementation, Custom development, and Product plus custom extension. Desktop arranges cards horizontally across a connected progress line with a subtle traveling pulse, featuring clean tabular numerals (01–04), Lucide category icons, and Specular Button hover sheens with slight 3D pointer tilt. Mobile collapses the layout into an ergonomic vertical stack with clear spacing and touch-scale press responses.

## S5 Services
The services section presents Kavirox's 11 core service lines plus a profile capability strip ("Also"). The layout features an asymmetric 3-column desktop grid with a prominent featured first card spanning 2 columns, integrated category chips (All, Commerce, Automation, Data & AI, Engineering) with counts, and an Expandable Search bar. Cards utilize 3D flip triggers to show deliverable checklists and tool badges on the reverse side, capped to avoid dead space, while eliminating all `//` ornaments. Underneath, a continuous auto-scrolling pill marquee displays broader company capabilities. Mobile presents an interactive snap rail with horizontal chip scrolling.

## S6 Solutions
Rebuilt as a high-density, clean sticky split layout on desktop (5/12 left, 7/12 right). The left column stays permanently sticky and anchored, presenting the section header, an interactive step indicator for the 4 solutions, and an active animated blueprint diagram (Input -> Engine -> Output) with a looping pulse. The right column scrolls through concise, readable solution cards containing three core architecture highlights, stack badges, Pixel Swap before/after interactive demonstration in Storefronts, and a "Targets we design for" metric trio with explanatory tooltip. Mobile renders as a vertical stack with embedded 3-step blueprint visual rows and 20px gutters.

## S7 Process
The "How a project runs" section details the 6-stage delivery lifecycle (`deliverySteps`), removing old e-commerce-only references in favor of product-neutral delivery terminology. Desktop features a sticky split view: the left panel presents prominent tabular step numerals and active step titles, while the right displays the 6 stages along a continuous scroll-drawn SVG connector line that progressively illuminates passed nodes. On mobile, it transforms into an elegant vertical stepper with an aligned left rule, clear touch targets, and reduced-motion static visibility.

## S8 About and Trust
Positioned as an honest company profile statement pairing narrative with verified institutional milestones. The left side presents the Kavirox company story in readable display typography, while the right features a clean bento grid of three factual proof cards: "Incubated at AIC-GBU", "Greater Noida, India", and the live count "7 products and projects in the portfolio". Designed using static frosted glass with 1px border highlights, avoiding unverified client metrics or fake logos.

## S9 FAQ
A two-column accordion on large displays (sticky section header left, accordion panels right; single-column on mobile) addressing core operational, architectural, and product questions (product vs. agency, security practices, warranties, live readiness). Panels expand smoothly using CSS grid-template-rows animation without layout thrashing, ensuring keyboard navigation accessibility, proper ARIA states, and zero cosmetic `//` symbols.

## S10 Contact
The contact portal centers around a Shine Border glass card backed on desktop by a subtle Soft Aurora field. Users can toggle between two explicit intent paths: "Discuss a project" (custom inquiry) and "Ask about a product" (dropdown selector populated with the 7 catalog items). All form inputs feature floating sentence-case labels, accessible placeholders, and validation. Submission triggers a Stateful Button with realistic feedback, generating an exact pre-formatted `mailto:` draft. On mobile, the aurora is disabled for performance, inputs span full width, and safe keyboard offsets prevent chat pill collisions.

## S11 Footer
A clean structural footer crowned by a large, artistically cropped Kavirox logo watermark along the upper border. Four structured columns—Products (links to all 7 detail routes), Services, Company, and Contact—provide clear navigation free of terminal ornaments. The footer integrates Uiverse squircle icon buttons for LinkedIn, Instagram, and email, respects device safe-area insets, and states the verified brand line: "BUILD. SECURE. GROW. — Incubated at AIC-GBU, Greater Noida".

## S12 Chatbot ("Ask Kavirox")
An intelligent assistant restyled to align with the product-led company profile. The launcher uses a 48px circular glass button on mobile and a compact pill on desktop. On mobile devices, the chat window presents as an 85dvh bottom sheet with swipe-down dismissal, backdrop blur, and touch handling. The internal header features the compact vector Kavirox mark, and the thinking state incorporates the Lattice Loader. Chatbot knowledge constants (`studio-knowledge.ts`) are synchronized with the 7 products, company positioning, and security policies.
