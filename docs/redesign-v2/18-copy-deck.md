## FAQ
- Eyebrow: Questions
- H2: Things people ask first.
- Q1 Is Kavirox a product company or an agency? A: "Both, in that order. We build our own products and also deliver technology services. The products show what we can do; the services fit it to your needs."
- Q2 Can I use a product as it is? A: "Where a product already covers your main need, yes. We explain scope, setup, limits, data handling, support and price before you decide."
- Q3 What if a product does not fit exactly? A: "We can configure it, integrate it with your tools, or extend it with custom modules, and we keep the standard and the custom parts clearly separate."
- Q4 Are all your products live today? A: "No. Each product shows its real status: prototype, in development, pilot or built. We do not present unfinished work as finished."
- Q5 How do you approach security? A: "We design to strengthen security and reduce exposure, using access control, data protection and testing. We do not promise that any system is completely safe. Security testing is scoped and done with authorization."
- Q6 Do you guarantee rankings, sales or leads? A: "No. We improve the foundations, such as speed, structure, tracking and experience, and we measure results, but we do not guarantee outcomes."
- Q7 How do we start? A: "Send us a short note about what you want to build. We reply with questions, a rough plan and the next step."
(Do not add pricing or timelines. If the repo's studio knowledge has verified answers, they may be added.)
