# 02 — Company, tagline and copy (source: Kavirox Master Company Profile, 7 Nov 2026)

## Positioning
Kavirox is a **product-led technology company with services**. Two connected parts: (1) its own products and platforms, (2) technology services to design, build, integrate, secure, launch and grow digital systems. Products prove the capability; services fit it to a customer's needs. Never describe Kavirox as only an agency.

## Brand line and descriptor
- Brand line: **BUILD. SECURE. GROW.**
- Descriptor under the logo: Technology • Products • Solutions (the logo artwork itself prints "Technology • Growth • Solutions"; use the profile wording in live text and log the mismatch for the owner)
- BUILD: products, SaaS, web and mobile apps, e-commerce systems, integrations, automation.
- SECURE: access control, data protection, application security, testing, reliability.
- GROW: discovery, usability, adoption, customer experience, efficiency, digital reach.

## Hero copy (use exactly)
- Headline: **Products that solve problems.** Second line, quieter: **Technology that moves businesses forward.**
- Accent (stroke-drawn) word in the headline: "solve".
- Supporting text: use the shorter version in `18-copy-deck.md` (it fits 2-3 lines).
- Primary button: **Explore Products** (scrolls to Products). Secondary: **Discuss a Project** (opens the existing pre-written email).
- Small line: "Incubated at AIC-GBU, Greater Noida".
- Brand line shown once, small, near the bottom of the hero: BUILD. SECURE. GROW.

We work across SaaS and product development, custom software, web and mobile applications, AI and automation, e-commerce systems, cybersecurity, cloud and DevOps, and digital growth. We develop our own products while helping customers design, build, integrate, deploy and improve technology that addresses real needs."

## Four ways to work with Kavirox (use as a 4-card section "How you can work with us")
- **Ready-built product** — Choose a Kavirox product that already addresses the main need. We explain scope, setup, limits, data handling, support and price.
- **Product implementation** — We configure, onboard users, connect data, set permissions, integrate with your platforms and train your team.
- **Custom development** — For needs no product covers: websites, applications, SaaS modules, integrations, automation, dashboards.
- **Product plus custom extension** — Start from a product and commission extra modules or integrations. We separate what is standard from what is custom.

## Responsible-claims rules (apply everywhere)
- Say "strengthen security", "identify vulnerabilities", "assess exposure". Never "100% protected" or "unhackable".
- VAPT: "scoped assessment performed with authorization".
- SEO and e-commerce: no guaranteed rankings, leads, conversion or revenue.
- AI: no "always accurate" or "hallucination-free". Say what sources it uses and when it hands off to a person.
- Metrics on the Solutions section stay labeled "Targets we design for".
- Do not claim endorsement by GBU or any institution.
